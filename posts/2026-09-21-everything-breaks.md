# Everything breaks — 17–21 September 2026

This is where the game became about keeping the boat afloat.

- **Driving from inside.** Doors, a dashboard with a console screen and a threat radar, and buttons you aim at and press.
  Rain on the windshield and working wipers.
- **Water in the boat.** Waves that come over the side and hull breaches now fill the deck. The crew has to get the
  water out.
- **Tools.** A hotbar with tools that work: a bucket to bail, a wrench to patch holes, an extinguisher, and a
  lifebuoy you can throw, then reel back in with someone holding on. New animations for swimming and reeling.
- **One long haul (20th).** The map became two big ports about 4 km apart across open sea, with a run director
  deciding when trouble arrives. Docking at the far port, fuel that can run out.
- **Progress (21st).** Cash, runs and best times are saved per machine. A tuning pass, recorded weather sounds,
  a toy-box style UI, a lobby card, solo difficulty scaling and multiplayer hardening.
