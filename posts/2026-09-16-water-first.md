# Water first — 14–16 September 2026

The game started with the sea, not the boat.

- **Waves.** The waves are made by stacking a set of simple wave shapes. The same wave data runs on the graphics card (to draw the
  sea) and on the CPU (so the boat knows how high the water is under each float point). Three sea presets, calm,
  default and storm, blend smoothly into each other.
- **Something to float.** A placeholder boat floats on probe points and has a chase camera. The first "disasters"
  were rogue waves, a tsunami and a whirlpool.
- **First playable crew boat (15th).** Bow-first handling, a spring-arm camera, an anchor, characters walking on the
  deck, a helm to sit at, engine and weather sounds.
- **A place to go (16th).** Islands with shores, storm visuals, the first run loop (deliver the crate, get a score),
  and a dock lobby where the crew gets ready. A new boat model went in the same day.
